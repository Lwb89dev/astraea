# Code of conduct

The Astraea project welcomes constructive participation from people of all
backgrounds and experience levels.

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good faith,
accept technical feedback, protect other people's privacy and keep discussion
focused on the project. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal
attacks, doxxing and publication of another person's private data are not
acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior harms the community or the security of its users. Serious conduct
issues should be reported privately through the repository's security-advisory
channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and with appropriate confidentiality.

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other
spaces officially associated with Astraea.
